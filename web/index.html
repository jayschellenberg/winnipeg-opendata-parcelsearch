About this tool

Search Winnipeg properties by civic address, roll number, or legal description. Each result is enriched with its top-2 zoning districts (with coverage %), every civic address falling inside the parcel polygon, and a per-parcel land area. Live data from the City of Winnipeg Open Data Portal — no server, no auth, every search re-queries the source. Browser-cached citywide zoning (7 days) and traffic volumes (30 days) keep repeat work in the same area snappy.

About the parcel layers

Survey parcels (blue on the map) are the legal lots from Land Titles — the Lot / Block / Plan on a deed.

Assessment parcels (yellow) are the City's tax-assessed properties — one per Roll Number / civic address.

The two layers don't always align. One assessment can cover many lots; one lot can be split between rolls. Hover anywhere on the map to see both at once.

Open-data sources

Live queries against the City of Winnipeg Open Data Portal (Socrata) — Survey Parcels, Assessment Parcels, Civic Addresses, the Zoning By-law, OurWinnipeg overlays (Precincts, Major Redevelopment Sites, Mature Community / Infill, Regional Mixed-Use Centres, Urban + Regional Corridors), and Traffic Counts (midblock + permanent stations) joined to the Road Network centerlines.

Environmental sites come from the Province of Manitoba Contaminated Sites Registry, fetched through a same-origin proxy because the upstream CSV doesn't send CORS headers.

External tools

Per-row links deep-link into: City of Winnipeg Assessment & Taxation, PP&D Property Map, DMIS (Development Management Information System), Walk Score, and the Manitoba Flood Mapping Tool. None require sign-in.

Basemaps

Protomaps / OpenStreetMap (Streets, default; self-hosted) and Esri World Imagery (Satellite). Toggle in the map's top-right corner. No API keys.

        How to search River Lots, Outer Two Mile, and Section-Township-Range parcels

        Unusual legal descriptions go in the same fields, just with different values:

        • River Lots: put RL in the Block field and the parish code in the Plan field (e.g. JA for St. James, CH for St. Charles, or another parish). The Lot field takes the river-lot number.
        • Outer Two Mile lots: put OT in the Block field and the parish code in the Plan field.
        • Section-Township-Range for a description like NW 10-11-4E (NW ¼ of Section 10, Township 11, Range 4E): put the section in Lot (10), the township in Block (11), the range in Plan (4E), and optionally the quarter (NW) in Description.

        Partial matches work in every field.
